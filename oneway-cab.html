NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

What is a one-way cab?

A one-way cab service allows you to pay only for the distance traveled to your destination without paying return charges.

Can I book from Zirakpur or Chandigarh?

Yes, we offer doorstep pickups from anywhere across Chandigarh, Mohali, Zirakpur, Panchkula, and airport zones.

Can I choose my vehicle?

Yes, options range from compact Sedans to Ertiga, Innova Crysta, and luxury Hycross models based on your group size.

How do I make the booking live?

Simply fill out the form above or click any route to connect directly with us on WhatsApp for instant confirmation.
